Instructions for adding your custom resume file:

1. Place your exported PDF file in this `public` folder and name it `resume.pdf` (e.g. `c:\Users\darsh\OneDrive\Desktop\portfolio\public\resume.pdf`).
2. Update `resumeUrl` in `src/data/portfolioData.js` to `/resume.pdf`.
3. Visitors will then be able to download your exact PDF directly!
